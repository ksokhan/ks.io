A few things I believe about design. Current as of Nov 2026. (This will probably change.)

Design is about understanding things. It’s only by getting our heads around all of the problem—the complexity, constraints, and interactions—that we get to the simple and elegant solution.

The secret is the conditions. My job is helping the people around me do their best work, so I optimize for trust, joy, experimentation, and doing that one last rep to get to something great.

Choose what to obsess over. Life’s too short to make anything but beautiful things, and also too short to chase perfection. I think a lot about how to pick the right spot on that spectrum.

Generalists are underrated. I think generalists are just relentlessly curious people who can’t help but explore code, product, research, and craft. I resist being pigeonholed into one discipline. Not everyone should work this way, but I love it.
